Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Note: this policy is written to match Dot Kolam as an offline drawing app with no accounts, ads, or analytics. If you add any of those later (or a different app collects more data), come back and update the relevant section below before publishing that update — Google Play requires this page to accurately reflect what each app actually does.

This Privacy Policy explains how Clown Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our applications, including Dot Kolam, and this website.

Who we are

Clown Studio is an app development organisation based in Andhra Pradesh, India. If you have questions about this policy or our apps, see the Contact page for how to reach us.

Information we collect

Dot Kolam is a drawing tool that works entirely on your device. In its current version:

  • Kolam designs you create are stored on your device only, unless you choose to export or share them yourself.
  • We do not require you to create an account to use the app.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal contact details within the app.
  • We do not track your location or browsing activity.

Permissions

Dot Kolam may request storage access on your device, used only to save and export the kolam designs you create so you can view, share, or print them. This permission is not used to read or collect any other files on your device.

Third-party services

Dot Kolam does not currently use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. The app is distributed through Google Play, which applies its own Google Play privacy practices as the platform provider.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, because we do not collect personal data through the app in the first place.

Data retention & deletion

Content you create in Dot Kolam stays on your device until you delete it yourself or uninstall the app. Since we don't store your designs on our servers, there is nothing further for us to delete on your behalf.

Children's privacy

Dot Kolam is a general-audience creative app and is not directed specifically at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our apps evolve. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to itsclownstudio@gmail.com — see the Contact page for full details.
